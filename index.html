        What ClinicFlow AI is for

        Clinics lose time and money when patients miss appointments without warning. Reception staff then phone everyone by hand, and the slot is usually wasted. ClinicFlow AI is a working prototype of an automation that deals with this end to end.

        Objectives

        • Reduce no-shows. Score every appointment for no-show risk, so staff know who needs a phone call and who only needs a message.
        • Save staff time. Draft SMS and email reminders automatically, in the patient's own language, so staff handle only the exceptions.
        • Handle replies safely. Read free-text replies in any language, sort them into confirm, reschedule, cancel or question, and flag anything that sounds urgent for a person. Staff approve every change.
        • Recover lost slots. When someone cancels, offer the slot to the waiting list straight away, matched by doctor and urgency.
        • Show the value. Put a number on the revenue at risk and the share a phone call can save, with assumptions the user can change.
        • Protect patient privacy. Send the AI only first name, doctor, time, patient instructions and language. Messages never name a diagnosis or visit type.

        Where AI is used

        • Writing reminders in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil or Telugu from a strict JSON prompt, with a template fallback.
        • Reading patient replies to find intent, urgency and a suggested response.
        • Drafting waiting-list offers for open slots.
        • Writing the morning brief for the front desk from the day's numbers.

        Limits

        • Nothing is sent. The app drafts messages and a person sends them.
        • The risk score is a transparent rule set for demonstration. It is not clinically validated.
        • All patients, phone numbers and emails are made up.
        • Data stays in this browser. A real deployment needs a patient database, consent records and an SMS or WhatsApp service.

        How the risk score works

        Start at 12. Add 22 for each missed visit (up to 3), 10 for a first visit, 8 for a routine visit and 6 for a hard-to-attend slot (before 9, after 4 pm or Monday). The cap is 97. A confirmed visit keeps 30 percent of its score.
